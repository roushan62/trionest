About TrioNest

One partner. Three disciplines.
One accountable handover.

TrioNest Spaces exists because most fit-out projects fail on coordination — not construction. Three separate contractors, three contracts, three opinions about whose scope a problem belongs to. We built TrioNest the other way: interiors, electrical and HVAC under one roof, one project manager and one standard.

Our story

Built from the ground up — trade by trade

TrioNest Spaces brings together dedicated verticals in Civil & Interiors, Electrical and HVAC Engineering, each led by specialists who sign off their own trade — drawings, materials, testing — while a single project manager owns the programme.

The result is a delivery model clients feel immediately: one fixed-cost proposal instead of three competing quotes, one weekly progress report instead of three versions of the truth, and one handover pack with every test record, warranty and as-built drawing in it.

What we stand for

Values you can verify on site

Accountability

One contract, one project manager, one handover. When a ceiling grid clashes with a duct route, we resolve it internally — not in a three-way vendor email chain.

Quality that's auditable

Every stage produces a signed record — material approvals, inspections, test reports. Quality is evidenced, not promised.

On-time honesty

Fixed-cost proposals, milestone payments and weekly reporting against a baseline programme. Slippage is visible in week two, not week ten.

Safety without shortcuts

Permit-to-work, lock-out/tag-out, dust and noise containment in occupied buildings — site safety is engineered into the programme, not bolted on.

Transparent costing

Scope is frozen at BOQ stage and priced as a fixed-cost proposal. Variations are quoted and approved in writing before any work proceeds.

PAN-India consistency

Multi-city rollouts run from a single programme — the same drawing set, the same QA/QC records and the same handover pack in every city.
