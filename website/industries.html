FAQ

Questions clients ask us

How long does a corporate office fit-out take?
Programme depends on area, shell condition and long-lead items rather than a fixed rule. We issue a baseline programme at kick-off after the survey and report weekly against it. The two items that most often set the critical path are HVAC equipment lead time and landlord approval for base-build interfaces — both are addressed in the first two weeks.
Can you work while our office is occupied?
Yes. Occupied-office works are sequenced zone by zone with defined out-of-hours and weekend working windows, dust and noise containment, protected access routes and temporary power and cooling where a zone is taken offline. The sequencing plan is agreed before work starts, not improvised on site.
Can you deliver a multi-city rollout?
Yes. We have delivered multi-city scope for CARS24 across Lucknow, Patna and other locations, and multi-city office scope for Centricity across Kolkata, Gwalior, Jabalpur and Mehsana. Rollouts run from a single specification and procurement plan, with site teams reporting into one programme.
Do you handle landlord and building-management approvals?
We prepare and submit the drawings, method statements and contractor documentation the building manager requires, and we coordinate base-build interfaces such as the supply point, fire system tie-in and core-drilling permissions. The client remains the signatory on the fit-out agreement.
What do we receive at handover?
As-built drawings for civil, electrical and HVAC, testing and commissioning reports, O&M manuals, warranty certificates, the asset list with makes and serial numbers, the signed snag closure, and a training session for your facilities team.
How do you price a project?
Scope is frozen at BOQ stage and priced as a fixed-cost proposal with a milestone payment plan. Progress is tracked against the baseline programme and reported weekly. Variations are quoted and approved in writing before work proceeds — no surprise invoices at the end.
