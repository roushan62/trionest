01 Civil & Interiors

Design · Fit-out · Refurbishment

Corporate interiors delivered from brief to handover: space planning, design development, civil works, joinery, ceilings, flooring and finishes — with electrical and HVAC coordinated into the same drawing set rather than bolted on afterwards.

What's included

  • Interior design & space planning — space programming against headcount and work-setting mix, test-fit layouts, concept design, 3D visualisation and a coordinated GFC drawing set covering layout, ceiling, flooring, joinery and elevations.
  • Turnkey corporate fit-outs — full scope from bare shell or warm shell to occupancy: civil, partitions, ceilings, flooring, joinery, glazing, painting, loose furniture coordination, signage and final cleaning, under one contract and one programme.
  • Workplace refurbishment & R&M — phased refurbishment of occupied offices, floor-by-floor or zone-by-zone, with out-of-hours working, dust and noise containment, and temporary services so business continues through the works.
  • Civil works, partitions, ceilings, flooring — blockwork and drywall partitions with acoustic and fire ratings, grid and gypsum ceilings, raised access floors, vinyl, carpet tile and stone flooring, waterproofing and pantry and washroom civil works.
  • Project management & quality handover — a single project manager, weekly progress reporting, stage-wise QA/QC inspection, tracked snag closure and a complete as-built and warranty document pack.

Typical projects

Corporate office fit-outs Multi-city branch rollouts Occupied-office refurbishment Retail stores & showrooms Reception, cafeteria & meeting suites

Our approach: the six-stage process front-loads design — survey and brief establish the shell condition and space programme, then concept and GFC drawings are frozen before the BOQ is priced, so your fixed-cost proposal is built on a complete drawing set.

02 Electrical

LT · Panels · Low-voltage · MEP

Electrical contracting from load schedule to commissioning: LT distribution, panels, cabling, earthing, lighting and low-voltage systems, executed by a dedicated electrical team and handed over with signed test records.

What's included

  • Electrification of corporate & industrial sites — complete electrical scope from the supply point onward: load assessment, distribution design, installation, testing and commissioning for offices, retail sites, warehouses and manufacturing facilities.
  • LT/HT panels, DBs, MCC, PCC, capacitor panels — panel specification, procurement from approved manufacturers, installation, termination and testing, including capacitor panels for power-factor correction.
  • Cabling, earthing, lighting, UPS systems — power and control cabling with cable schedules and routing drawings, earthing with measured earth resistance, lux-level lighting design, and UPS and raw-power distribution for IT and critical loads.
  • Low-voltage: data, voice, CCTV, fire alarm — structured cabling and racks, voice and data outlets, CCTV, access control, public address and fire detection and alarm, coordinated with the interior ceiling and containment layout.
  • Sub-stations and electrical AMC / FMS — sub-station works, plus post-handover electrical AMC covering preventive maintenance, thermography, panel servicing and breakdown response under defined SLAs.

Typical projects

Office & delivery-centre electrification Industrial plant & warehouse electrical Retail lighting packages Panel supply & testing ELV system installation Electrical AMC & FMS

Our approach: design and BOQ produce the load schedule, single-line diagram, DB schedule and lighting layout coordinated against the ceiling plan before pricing. Procurement releases panels and long-lead switchgear first. Testing and commissioning covers insulation resistance, earth resistance, phase sequence, load and panel functional checks — all issued as signed test reports.

03 HVAC Engineering

Design · Install · AMC · Retrofit

HVAC engineering with in-house design: heat-load calculation, equipment selection, installation, balancing and commissioning — backed by 100+ installations and manufacturer warranty support.

What's included

  • VRF / VRV, ducted split & chiller systems — system selection matched to the building, occupancy pattern and operating budget: VRF/VRV for zoned offices, ducted split for smaller or phased areas, chiller-based systems for larger or continuous loads.
  • Greenfield HVAC turnkey & retrofit — new installations from design through commissioning, and retrofit of existing systems in occupied buildings with phased changeover so cooling is not lost while new plant is installed.
  • In-house CAD design & heat-load calculations — room-by-room heat-load calculation against occupancy, equipment, glazing and orientation, then equipment selection, duct and piping layout, drainage and ventilation design as coordinated CAD drawings.
  • Manufacturer-backed warranty & 24×7 AMC — equipment supplied through manufacturer channels so OEM warranty applies, registered at commissioning, with AMC covering preventive maintenance and breakdown response.
  • Operating with strict SLAs PAN-India — defined response and resolution times, scheduled preventive visits, filter and coil servicing, refrigerant and performance checks, and service records maintained per site.

Typical projects

Corporate office HVAC (VRF / VRV) Industrial & manufacturing HVAC Hospital & healthcare HVAC Retail & hospitality air conditioning HVAC retrofit & replacement HVAC AMC & breakdown support

Our approach: the survey verifies space for plant, routing and outdoor unit placement before anything is selected. Design produces the heat-load calculation and equipment schedule that drive the BOQ. Commissioning includes airflow balancing, refrigerant charge verification, temperature and control checks, and a signed T&C report with O&M manuals and warranty certificates.

04 AMC & Facility Management

Preventive · Breakdown · SLA-driven

Post-handover support across all three verticals: preventive maintenance schedules, breakdown response under defined SLAs, and service records that keep your asset auditable.

What's included

  • Comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC — comprehensive cover including spares and consumables, or labour-only cover where you hold spares; scoped per asset list at the end of the warranty period so there is no coverage gap.
  • Preventive maintenance schedules — a dated schedule per asset: HVAC filter, coil and refrigerant checks, panel and DB inspection with thermography, earthing checks, lighting and emergency-lighting testing, and interior R&M rounds.
  • Breakdown response under SLA — defined response and resolution times by fault severity, a logged ticket per call, and escalation to the relevant vertical head when a fault is not closed within the agreed window.
  • Facility management support — on-site or visiting technician deployment, minor civil and interior repairs, plumbing and pantry maintenance, and coordination with landlord and base-building teams.
  • Documentation and reporting — updated asset register, completed PM checklists, breakdown history and periodic service reports, so your facilities team can evidence maintenance during audits.

Typical projects

HVAC AMC — offices, hospitals, industrial Electrical AMC — panels, DBs, earthing Interior R&M contracts Multi-site facility management Post-warranty conversion

Our approach: AMC scoping starts with a survey of installed assets and their condition, followed by a proposed schedule and SLA matrix rather than a flat annual fee. Every visit produces a signed checklist, and the asset register is updated as equipment is serviced or replaced.
