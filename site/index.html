How we work

A six-stage delivery lifecycle

From the first site visit to the signed handover pack — every stage produces a deliverable you can hold us to.

Free Survey & Brief

On-site assessment of shell condition, load, HVAC provision and access — plus your brief, headcount and target date.

Deliverables:
  • Site measurement record
  • Requirement brief
  • Feasibility note & budget range

Design & BOQ

Concept, 3D visualisation, GFC drawings and MEP coordination — then a fixed-cost BOQ built on a complete drawing set.

Deliverables:
  • Concept & 3D visuals
  • Coordinated GFC set
  • Fixed-cost BOQ & programme

Approvals & Procurement

Landlord and authority approvals, material approvals with samples, and early release of long-lead equipment.

Deliverables:
  • Approved drawings
  • Material approvals
  • Procurement schedule

Execution

Stage-wise construction with weekly progress reports against baseline, and QC inspections at every milestone.

Deliverables:
  • Weekly progress reports
  • Stage-wise inspection records
  • Tracked snag list

Testing & Commissioning

Electrical testing, HVAC balancing and commissioning, functional checks — every test documented and signed.

Deliverables:
  • Test & commissioning reports
  • Balancing records
  • O&M manuals

Handover & Support

As-built drawings, warranties, signed snag closure and a training session for your facilities team — then AMC if you want it.

Deliverables:
  • As-built drawing pack
  • Warranty certificates
  • AMC proposal
